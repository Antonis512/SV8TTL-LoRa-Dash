# SV8TTL LoRa Dash

SV8TTL LoRa Dash is a standalone Android app for using MeshCore and Meshtastic nodes through Bluetooth or USB serial. It supports simultaneous connections and works without a Windows computer or TCP radio connection.

- **Chat:** exchange channel and direct messages, reply, search saved conversations, use message templates, share phone positions, and view delivery information. Two viewing slots let you switch between connected nodes.
- **Persistent sends:** retry a message at intervals of at least two minutes until confirmation, expiry, cancellation or an optional retry limit. Repeat phone-location sharing after movement of at least 100 metres and the configured interval.
- **Contacts:** keep profiles, photos, callsigns, notes and node assignments; import or link Android contacts; filter contacts with a group dropdown.
- **Nodes and signal:** search and filter discovered nodes, inspect reception details, manage favorites, and request supported information or route checks. Signal Quality shows confirmed zero-hop RF reports.
- **Map:** display node positions and shared-location trails with arrows from older to newer reports. Use offline maps, saved marks, overlays and geofences. Received map history has a rolling 30-day retention period.
- **Member mode:** provide a simpler Chat screen with prepared contacts and channels for less experienced users.
- **Sending controls:** split long messages within supported limits, send to selected destinations, and pause or continue sending chains.
- **Notifications:** configure sounds and silence alerts globally, by contact or by node.
- **Records and backups:** keep notes and templates, export conversations, and export or merge app backups.
- **Language:** select English or Greek in Config. Names, message contents and technical identifiers remain unchanged; unmapped interface text falls back to English.
