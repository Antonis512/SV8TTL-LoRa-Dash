# 0.10.25 fixes — 5 November 2026

Reported: Bluetooth connection problems with both MeshCore and Meshtastic, connected devices not automatically appearing in Chat, a geofence node differing from the official app, and Contacts buttons jumping the page.

## Changes

- BLE uses one read/write operation at a time. Meshtastic notifications received during a write retain a pending read; queued writes resume after a read. Previously the independent read/write flags allowed overlapping GATT operations and a rejected read could silently stop initial loading.
- Repeated MTU callbacks cannot repeat notification setup or signal readiness twice. Callback byte arrays are copied before posting to the Android main thread. Old queued sends and callbacks cannot affect a replacement connection.
- Connection, discovery, MTU, subscription, read and write operations have a 30-second watchdog with a stage-specific diagnostic. Failed reads and rejected writes close the unusable connection. Commands are not automatically resent.
- Empty default Chat panes select a connected radio of the corresponding family once its identity arrives. An explicit selection, including No device, is preserved; saved selections still resolve to their matching connected session.
- Contacts preserves scroll while rebuilding the same editor or detail/list view. Moving to another dialog screen starts at the top.
- Map deduplication prefers a valid positioned record over a copy without coordinates, then selects the newest positioned record. Equator/prime-meridian coordinates are valid; out-of-range and zero/zero coordinates are not plotted. Exact receiving-device filters remain respected.
- Short-viewport composer controls are compacted; in short landscape viewports their action buttons sit in a row. The reply textarea stays above the bottom navigation in the tested portrait/landscape/keyboard-sized viewports.
